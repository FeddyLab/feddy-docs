---
title: Feddy documentation
description: In-app support for iOS apps and websites. Users write in from inside your product; replies land back where they asked.
---

Feddy gives the people using your app or website a place to write in, and gives you one inbox to answer from. Replies show up inside the product, and by email when you have set up a sending domain. An assistant answers repeat questions from answers you approved, and hands everything else to you.


## Add Feddy to your product

- [iOS quickstart](/docs/quickstart/ios/): Swift Package, iOS 15 and later, no dependencies.
- [Web quickstart](/docs/quickstart/web/): one script tag, no build step.

## Learn how it works

- [Projects](/docs/concepts/projects/): one project per app or site, and the public project ID.
- [Conversations](/docs/concepts/conversations/): statuses, topics, notes, and attachments.
- [Contacts](/docs/concepts/contacts/): anonymous and identified users, and what you know about them.
- [Assistant](/docs/concepts/assistant/): keyword answers, AI answers, and the Copilot in your inbox.
- [Team](/docs/concepts/team/): members, notifications, and replying by email.

## Working with an AI coding agent

Every project's **Settings → Install** page has a prompt you can paste into your agent. It contains your project ID and the steps on this site. These docs are also indexed on Context7 and available as `llms.txt`.
