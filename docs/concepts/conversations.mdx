---
title: Conversations
description: What a thread between you and a user contains, what its status means, and what happens on its own.
---

A conversation is one thread between you and one user. It starts the moment a user sends a message and holds everything that follows: their messages, your replies, notes your team leaves for each other, and every change of status, in the order they happened.

Each conversation comes with context you didn't have to ask for: the topic the user picked, what device and app version they're on, and anything you've told Feddy about them. You'll find all of it in the panel next to the thread.

From the inbox you can:

- Reply to the user, or leave a note only your team can see.
- Change the status, or let Feddy change it for you as the conversation moves along.
- See the user's device, app version, and details, without asking them.
- Insert a saved reply by typing `/`.
- Mark a conversation unread so a teammate takes a look.

## Status

A conversation is always in one of three states. Think of it as "whose turn is it?"

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Open | It's your turn. New conversations start here, and a user replying to something you sent brings it back here. |
| Pending | It's the user's turn. Sending a reply moves the conversation here on its own. |
| Closed | Done. Closed conversations never show as unread. |

You can set any status yourself from the conversation header. Two things also happen without you:

- A conversation that sits in **Pending** for 14 days closes on its own. The user never answered, so there's nothing left to do.
- When a user answers "Was this helpful?" with yes after an assistant reply, the conversation closes as resolved.

If a user writes again within 7 days of a close, the same conversation reopens. After 7 days their message starts a fresh conversation, with a link back to the old one so you still have the history.

## Topics

When a user starts a conversation, they pick what it's about. This sorts your inbox before you've read a word.

Four topics are built in: **Bug**, **Feature request**, **Question**, and **Other**. Users see them in their own language. You can turn any of them off in **Settings → Topics**, but you can't rename or delete them.

Add your own for anything specific to your product, such as **Billing** or **Sync**. A custom topic has a name, translations for other languages, and a short code the SDK uses to refer to it. The code can't be changed once the topic exists, so pick it with care.

## What comes with each conversation

The SDK sends along what it can see about the user's setup, and the inbox shows it beside the thread:

- **iOS**: device model, iOS version, app version and build, and the device language.
- **Web**: the page they were on, browser, operating system, window size, where they came from, and the browser language.

If you've told Feddy who the user is, their name and details appear here too. See [Contacts](/concepts/contacts).

## Replies and notes

The box at the bottom of a conversation has two modes:

- **Reply** goes to the user. Sending one moves the conversation to **Pending**.
- **Note** stays inside your inbox. Use it to leave context for a teammate. The user never sees notes and nothing about the conversation changes.

<Tip>
Type `/` in the reply box to insert a saved reply. Set them up under **Settings → Saved replies**.
</Tip>

## Attachments

Users can attach up to 5 images to a message: PNG, JPEG, or WebP, up to 10 MB each. The SDK shrinks large images before uploading, so a photo from a phone camera is fine. Other kinds of files aren't accepted.

Attachments are available on paid plans.

## Unread

A conversation is unread when nobody on your team has seen its latest message yet. Opening it marks it read for everyone. If you want it to stand out again, use **Mark unread** in the header. Closed conversations never count as unread.
