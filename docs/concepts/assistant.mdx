---
title: Assistant
description: Automatic replies built from answers you approve, and a Copilot that drafts replies for you in the inbox.
---

The assistant does two jobs. It answers users on its own, within limits you set, and it helps you write replies in the inbox. It never makes up facts about your product: everything it says to a user comes from answers you wrote or approved.

You'll use it to:

- Answer the questions you get over and over, without touching them.
- Let users know they've been heard while they wait for you.
- Draft, shorten, translate, or soften a reply before you send it.

## Answers

An answer is the reply you'd give to a common question, saved once so it can be reused. Each one has a title for your own reference, and optionally keywords, a topic, and a platform that narrow when it applies. Write them under **Settings → Assistant → Knowledge**, or import a batch.

The same page has an **About your app** box: a short description the assistant reads before every reply. Fill it in. It's how the assistant knows what your product is and what it isn't.

## Modes

Choose how the assistant replies to users under **Settings → Assistant**:

| Mode | What users get |
| --- | --- |
| Keyword answers only | An answer whose keyword appears in their message. Anything else gets the fallback message. |
| Answer with AI | A reply composed from your answers. If your answers don't cover the question, the user gets the fallback message and the conversation waits for you. |
| Suggest replies to me | Nothing automatic beyond keyword answers. Instead, the AI drafts a reply for you in the inbox. |

Turn **Enabled** off to send nothing automatically.

Whatever the mode, the assistant never changes a conversation's status and never marks it resolved. Only you, or the user, can close a conversation.

## Fallback message

The fallback message goes out when a new conversation gets no answer, so the user knows they've been heard and roughly when to expect you. The default is a thank-you with a 24 to 48 hour expectation; change it to match your real response time. Leave it empty to send nothing.

## AI instructions

Free text the AI follows on every reply: the tone to use, what to call your product, things it should never say. Keep facts in your answers and use instructions for behavior.

## Was this helpful?

After an assistant reply, the user sees a **Was this helpful?** prompt. Yes closes the conversation as resolved. No leaves it open and flags the reply in your inbox so you can step in. **Results** on the Knowledge page shows how often replies were sent, rated helpful, resolved, or handed over to you.

## Learn from closed conversations

When this is on, the assistant reads conversations you close and drafts new answers from them, listed under **Suggested answers** for you to review. Nothing becomes an answer until you approve it.

## Copilot

Next to every conversation, the **Copilot** tab has already read the thread, your notes, and your answers. Ask it to draft a reply, summarize, translate into the user's language, or soften the tone. What it writes goes into your reply box, where you edit it and send it yourself. Nothing reaches the user until you press send.

## Try it

**Try it** on the Knowledge page runs a message through the assistant exactly as a user would see it, with a topic, platform, and language of your choosing. Nothing is sent to anyone.

## Availability

AI answers and the Copilot are available on paid plans. Keyword answers and the fallback message work on every plan.
